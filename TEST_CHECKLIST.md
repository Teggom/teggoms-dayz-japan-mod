# Japan test island: Phase C wave 1 walk (~28 min)

Wave 1 is built: every town and rural type now has a furnished version (furniture, the life layer, loot on floors
and on furniture), the test street and the hamlet are dressed, and there are two new storehouses (kura) with an
upper floor. This is ONE walk through all of it. Pictures of everything below:
`research/production/contact_sheets/c3_rooms.jpg`, `c3_street.jpg`, `c3_hamlet.jpg`, `c3_kura.jpg`, `c3_plans.jpg`
(loot plans).

**Start:**
1. Run `start-japan-test-island.bat` in the server folder. It starts only the test server, on its own port, so the
   live server is unaffected.
2. When the server is up, run `start-japan-test-client.bat` to join.

**If it won't load, kicks you, or something is invisible:** just tell me. I read the server and client logs myself,
so you don't need to open any log.

**Directions** are from the spawn. You spawn facing north; the furnished machiya shop is ~55 m ahead. The **hamlet**
is ~80 m to your front-left (north-west). The **town street** runs east-west ~95 m ahead, behind the machiya.

**Not in this walk:** zombies inside buildings (the navmesh is not regenerated yet, so they can't path indoors).

## 1. The hamlet (10 min)

Walk north-west to the thatched houses. There is a threshing yard in the middle with a big straw stack.

- **Kanto farmhouse** (the big hipped-thatch house at the back left, facing you):
  - **Its look first.** C2 raised its walls 0.42 m and gave it no skirt roof over the door, because a skirt roof
    can't fit under the thatch eave. **Does the house look too tall / leggy from the yard?**
  - Persimmons hang under the front eave; tools lean by the big plank door.
  - Go in through the big plank door (left end). Earth-floored doma: a two-mouth clay stove with a pot and a pot
    with no lid, the horse stall in the back corner with its manger and rotting straw, a rice mortar, rice bales by
    the door, a spilled winnowing basket.
  - Step up onto the board floor (the stone by the step). Irori hearth with the pot hook over it, persimmons and
    daikon drying from the beam, a box-tray meal by the hearth, a lamp, a shelf of trays, the Buddhist shelf.
  - The two small rooms behind: the best room (tatami: long chest, lacquered box, cushions) and the bedroom
    (bedding laid out, clothes rack, spinning wheel, an open wicker trunk).
  - Outside at the west end: the stable yard (tie post, pack saddle on a rack, stone trough).
- **Kinai farmhouse** (the tall house to the right with the tiled lower roofs):
  - Front door at its right end. Earth floor with the ox stall (empty manger), the stove, a stone hand mill.
  - Front room: a ground loom with cotton on it. Back room: irori with an abandoned hook, bowls scattered.
    Best room and bedroom on the far end.
- **The huts across the yard** (south side, doors facing the farmhouses):
  - Facing them from the yard: the **left** one (east hut, board floor) and the **middle** one (west hut, with an
    open woodshed lean-to on its right) are furnished, poor and sparse: a one-mouth stove, a straw bed (no tatami, no futon), the irori.
  - The **right** one (small, straw-mat doorway) is left bare on purpose.
- **Sheds behind the farmhouses:** the right one (walled, stone-weighted roof, woodshed lean-to) has bales,
  shelving and a winnow inside; the open thatched one on the left is bare.
- **Around the yard:** rice drying racks, stooks and a scarecrow in the fields, persimmons and daikon on poles,
  a bamboo pipe running into a trough between the farmhouses, a **lever well** by the huts. Crouch at the lever
  well: does **Drink** show? Does a bottle fill?

## 2. The two kura (6 min)

- **Hamlet kura** (white with black boards, at the east edge of the hamlet, door facing the hamlet):
  - **The plaster door leaves are real doors** (the first hinged doors on any of our buildings). They start open.
    Close both, then open them again, from outside. Do they swing the right way (outwards, away from the wall)?
    Do they clip into the stepped plaster surround?
  - Then the inner sliding plank door: open, walk in, close it behind you, open it again.
  - Inside: chests, bales, stacked boxes. **Climb the stair** along the back wall to the upper floor. Does it walk
    smoothly? Upstairs: an open long chest, shelving, toppled boxes; the guard rail round the stairwell; the small
    barred windows in both gables.
- **Town kura** (all white with the diagonal tile pattern, behind the north row of the street; from the hamlet walk
  north-east to it, ~60 m): its outer leaves are fixed open (a static version). Same stair and upper floor. Walk the
  stone step at the door up and down.

## 3. The town street (10 min)

Walk out to the street (the rows of townhouses, east-west).

- **The north row, west part (Kamigata):** from the west, a bare end unit, the **paper shop** (noren, a
  brush-shaped sign), the **rice dealer** (noren, a hanging signboard), a bare corner unit.
  - Go into the rice dealer: the earth passage (toriniwa) runs to the back; the shop room on the left has rice bales,
    straw sacks, the account desk with its coin box, a spilled measure; the back room has laid bedding, a chest, a
    lamp and the Buddhist altar; the kitchen at the back has the stove (pots in both mouths), sink, shelf and water
    jar. Do the same in the paper shop (smaller, 2 ken).
  - **Seams:** look along the row from the street and from the back: where the units meet (pent ends, the fire
    walls between Kamigata units), any gaps, flicker, or light through a party wall from inside?
- **The south row (facing north):** the **post-town house** (west end: a lived-in home, bench and sandals for sale
  under its eave), two bare row houses, then the **inn** (lantern, bench, sandals for sale) and the **grand inn**.
  - Inn: the office with the counting desk and its lattice, the entrance screen; two guest rooms with bedding and
    left-over meals; the big kitchen with a row of three stove mouths.
  - **Grand inn upstairs:** go in, through the back room behind the office, and **climb the box stair**. Walk both
    upstairs guest rooms (bedding, a dragged-off futon, a go board with the stones scattered). Open the upstairs
    street windows.
- **The north row, east part (Edo):** a bare end unit, a bare board-roof unit, the **cloth dealer**, and the **sake
  shop** on the corner (casks on a rack, flasks, the fire tub round the side corner).
- **The ward corner** (north side, between the two rows): the fire-watch ladder with its bell, the bucket rack,
  the notice board, a crossroads lantern, a Jizo. **In the street:** a tipped palanquin, a dropped hat and stick, a
  burst bundle, spilled vendor loads, a fallen shop shutter, scattered clogs, torn lanterns, a broken handcart.

## 4. Everywhere, as you go (2 min of attention)

- Anything **floating, sinking or see-through** (furniture, pots on the stoves, things on shelves, the gutter covers
  in front of the shops, lanterns and curtains under eaves). Gutter covers sit low on purpose (they belong in a
  ditch the terrain doesn't have yet).
- **Doors:** every door opens and closes from both sides; nothing blocks a door.
- **Walking:** can you get through every room without snagging on furniture? Any furniture you can't step past?
- **Loot:** does loot show up on floors AND on furniture (shelf boards, chest tops, bales, the desk, the stove top
  is not one)?
