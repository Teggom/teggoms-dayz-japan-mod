# Japan test island: G4 re-check after the F1 fixes (~10 min)

Your G4 walk found 8 things plus the well. All 8 are fixed and rebuilt, and the well got a likely fix. This is a short
look at exactly those spots. Before/after pictures: `spikes/F1/f1_fixes.jpg`.

**Start:**
1. Run `start-japan-test-island.bat` in the server folder. It starts only the test server, on its own port, so the
   live server is unaffected.
2. When the server is up, run `start-japan-test-client.bat` to join.

**If it won't load, kicks you, or something is invisible:** just tell me. I read the server and client logs myself.
The well also writes a test line to the script log at start-up; I read that too, so you don't need to open any log.

**Directions** are from the spawn. You spawn facing north; the furnished shop is ~55 m ahead, entrance under the long
blue noren. The yard is behind the house.

## 1. Street front (2 min)

- **Noren (fix 1):** stand close to the long blue noren on the entrance. The cloth should look like plain woven
  cotton all the way down to the hem, with the white shop mark near the top of the middle panel. There should be no
  vertical streaks.
- **Fire tub (fix 2):** the big open tub at the left front corner of the house. Walk right up to it and look down
  inside at the leaves, especially where they meet the far wall. Is there any sliver where you can see through or
  see light? Walk round it and try a few angles.

## 2. Inside (4 min)

- **Goods stand (fix 8):** in the shop room (off the passage, on the street side). Climb onto the stepped stand and
  stand on each step, then on the top.
  - Do you stand still, or jitter?
  - Also try climbing onto: the long chest (storage room, climb in); the rice bales; the chest of drawers top
    (best room); the box stack (storage corner).
- **Kitchen stove (fix 5):** the pot with no lid sits in the stove. Its lid now lies on the earth floor at the end of
  the stove. Is anything floating?
- **God shelf (fix 6):** the small shrine high on the kitchen wall. You should see its roof slope facing you, with the
  door under the eave. The ridge runs left to right, with three little logs across it and crossed boards at the
  ends. Before, it showed its pointed gable end to you.
- **Cloth over the chests (fix 7):**
  - In the storage room, the long chest with its lid thrown back. The blue garment should go over the front edge and
    down inside onto the pale cloth lying in the chest. On the outside, it should hang flat against the front.
  - In the best room, the garment on the pulled drawer of the chest of drawers should also hug the drawer front.
  - Does any cloth float?

## 3. The yard (3 min)

Go out the back door.
- **Laundry pole (fixes 3 and 4):** the blue cloth is now a kimono, T-shaped, hung with the pole through both
  sleeves. Look at the top of each forked post from the side: the V should open across the pole, with the pole lying
  in it.
- **The well:** crouch at the stone curb, facing the water.
  1. **Drink.**
  2. **Fill the WaterBottle.** It's on the item grid by the spawn, at about (1019.5, 975).
  3. **Wash hands** only shows when your hands are bloody. Skip it unless they happen to be.
