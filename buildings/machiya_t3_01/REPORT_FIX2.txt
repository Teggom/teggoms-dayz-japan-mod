  environment reflection at grazing angles, not the sun specular. The SMDI map does not keep it off.

      environment map at all.

1.2 Pulls on the wrong edge
  Rule the code followed: none. openings.leaf_plank put the iron pull at the leaf's l1 edge, whatever way the leaf slid.
  Every plank leaf slides +x in its part, so the pull sat on the LEADING edge, which parks behind the wall or the fixed
  panel. The house's yaw 180 and the mirrored parts (back door, windows) move the pull and the slide together, so they
  were not the cause. The kura inner door had the same bug.
  Correct rule (PLAYBOOK T10): the pull goes on the TRAILING edge, the one that closes against the jamb and keeps the
  0.22 m STUB in the doorway when open. It sits 0.06-0.14 m in from that edge, is 10 mm proud, clears a leaf on the
  next track (12 mm), and is tagged "pull".
  Where:
    jpparts/openings.py   pull_x(); leaf_plank, leaf_shoji, leaf_lattice and kura inner take dirn; _leaf_set
                          passes it
    jpparts/core.py       sliding_leaf passes dirn=direction to the leaf builder
  Affected on the house: the entrance (both leaves), the back door, and kitchen -> storage. These are the only leaves
  with pulls; shoji, the amado and the window panels have none.
  Renders (looked at): renders/fix2_entrance_closed.png / _open.png and fix2_back_door_closed.png / _open.png. Closed,
  the pull is by the post the leaf closes on. Open, it stays in the doorway stub.

1.3 See-through slit at the jamb
  Cause:
    - A closed leaf overlapped its post by only OV = 2 cm while running 1.2 cm (inner track) to 6.4 cm (outer track)
      in front of the post face.
    - At the entrance's closing post (Stephen's "post on the left" from inside), the outer leaf left a 6.4 cm deep
      slot, visible from about 17 degrees off the wall normal.
    - The single doors had the same slot at the fixed panel's stile (4 cm).
    - The hikichigai and hikiwake meeting stiles overlapped 2 cm across a 1.2 cm gap.
    - Leaf boards had board_run's 4 mm gaps straight through the leaf (nothing behind them in Resolution 1): daylight
      lines through every closed plank leaf and the amado.
  Fix (kit, every building):
    - jamb_stop(): at the CLOSING jamb, a 45 mm stop on the post, from the wall face out past the outermost leaf, 2 mm
      off the leaf edge.
    - jamb_lip(): on a post or fixed stile a leaf SLIDES PAST, a lip filling the gap under the leaf (2 mm clearance).
    - MEET = 0.05 m meeting-stile overlap for hikichigai and hikiwake. Leaves are +1.5 cm wide (0.895 m, still inside
      the 0.85-0.95 period width).
    - LEAF_GAP = 0: leaf boards butt tight (leaf_plank plain and battened, kura inner, tsukiage).
    - Stops and lips are 3-face visual strips in Resolution 1 and 2, with no collision. The sweep, clear-width and
      reach checks are unchanged and pass.
  Used by: twin_leaves (hikichigai doors and the amado window), split_leaves (hikiwake: lips on both posts),
  single_door_part, sliding_door_part. The window_slide panels needed nothing: the lattice or bars outside already
  seal them (C17 0).
  Renders (looked at): fix2_entrance_jamb_inside.png (Stephen's view, closing post) and fix2_entrance_lip_inside.png
  (the other post). No light line.

  - Kawara, namako tile and iron keep their finish unchanged: Stephen passed the roof and the far LOD.

    defect class.
