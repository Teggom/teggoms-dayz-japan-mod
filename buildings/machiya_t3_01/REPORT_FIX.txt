1. WHAT CHANGED, PER DEFECT
---------------------------

1. "Roof tiles are not on the roof; there's a gap when I get close"
   Cause: the kawara bed plane sat 0.152 above the rafter plane, but the sheathing top was at 0.072. That left an 8 cm
   void, open at every eave and verge.
   Fix (kit, every tile roof):
   - roofs.tile_bed() adds a fuki-tsuchi clay bed (the arakabe earth) from the sheathing top to the tiles.
   - roofs.kawara_fascia() adds a fascia board (kayaoi) at every eave, square to the rafters. Its top is 12 mm above
     the tile bed plane, under the eave tiles, whose lips now hang in front of it.
   Where it is used:
   - cover_kawara, so every roofs.roof()
   - roofparts.pent (street and gable pents)
   - the kura mini-pents and the kura eave
   - the field samples
   - the machiya lean-to
   Check: C13 (3,644/3,644 field points on the bed; 5 eaves, 5 fascias).

2. "Doors can only be closed from one direction"
   Research, from the vanilla scripts and ODOLs:
   - A door is targeted only through the View Geometry component that the camera ray hits: 5 m,
     ObjIntersectView -> Building.GetDoorIndex.
   - The hit point and the leaf's memory point must both be within 2 m (CCTCursor and IsInReach).
   - Vanilla sliding leaves never clear the opening: 0.22-0.5 m stays in it.
   Fix (kit):
   - Every sliding leaf now stops with core.STUB = 0.22 m of its broad face inside the opening.
   - The leaf memory point moves to the trailing edge, as in vanilla.
   - Every leaf has View Geometry (the open-bar koshido leaf had none).
   Knock-on fixes:
   - The kura door opening was widened to 1.24 m (so it still clears 1.02).
   - The kura outer plaster leaves now meet at the centre and swing 90 deg, not 100 deg (they had overlapped 8 cm and
     swung back into the surround).
   - The suriagedo boards stop 0.22 m below the head.
   Check C10 aims the camera ray at the leaf's broad faces from 15 standing positions per side, open and closed. The
   far side must hit inside the doorway. It reproduces the old defect (0/15 from the far side) and all 10 doors and
   windows now pass: 13-15/15 per side.
   Measured clear widths: entrance 1.46 m, hikiwake 1.24 m, singles 1.06 m (D1 >= 1.00).

3. Door variety
   New parts (sheet contact_sheets/3c_openings_g3.jpg):
   - jp_p_open_itado_single, jp_p_open_shoji_ext_single: katabiki, one ~1.34 m leaf beside a fixed half-panel.
   - jp_p_open_shoji_ext_hikiwake: two leaves parting in the middle.
   On the house:
   - entrance: hikichigai (the twin, main entrance only)
   - mise <-> zashiki: hikiwake
   - toriniwa -> zashiki and toriniwa -> mise: single shoji (their stepping stones re-centred on the narrower opening)
   - back door and kitchen -> storage: single itado
   Where each style belongs: PLAYBOOK §15 T3.

4. "From inside I can see outside" (the tatami room)
   The C11 ray check found two slits:
   - (a) The degoshi head board stopped 2 cm under the wall's head rail, so there was a slit at 2.25-2.27 m: eye height
     for someone standing on the 0.50 m tatami.
   - (b) A diagonal slit where the toriniwa's outside board wall met the plaster wall above (2.2 m).
   Fixes (kit):
   - The degoshi head board now runs up to the head rail and back to the wall's inner face (Geometry, View and Fire).
   - Every board_vertical wall run gets a top rail across its full thickness.
   Pent soffit: C11 shows the street pent was already closed. No leak ran through it, so no soffit board was needed.
   The note for future models is PLAYBOOK §15 T5, and the check is C11 (rooms: 0 leaks; 17 rays leave through door
   and window portals, which is allowed).

5. Openable windows
   New parts, all animated like doors, all engine-untested:
   - jp_p_open_window_slide_shoji / _board: a sliding panel behind a koshi lattice or renji bars.
   - jp_p_open_amado_window_twin: two storm shutters stowing in a tobukuro box.
   - jp_p_open_tsukiage_board: a top-hinged push-up shutter (rotation, 75 deg).
   On the house:
   - DoorsTwin7: the street window (B2b)
   - DoorsTwin8: the zashiki gable window (replaces the koshi; the 2.5-ken post was removed)
   - DoorsTwin9: the kitchen window (replaces the static renji)
   - DoorsTwin10: the storage gable window (sill 1.18 m above the storage floor, clear of the wainscot)
   Rotation sense: +angle1 turns by the right-hand rule about axis point 1 -> 2. This was read off 10 vanilla vehicle
   doors, hoods and trunks. build.py now writes type="rotation" / angle1 for rotation leaves.

6. Interior walls dark and greenish
   New material jp_m_wall_nakanuri_int (palette earth_wall_aged, warmer, unweathered, about the same lightness; mean
   (127,99,75), dE 6.6/14).
   - walls.wall_run(interior='back'|'both') puts it on every face that looks into a room. The house uses it on
     exterior walls (the inside face), partitions (both faces) and the kamado.
   - Check: C14 (no exterior-weathered earth face looks into a room).

7. Roof LOD pops
   (a) Too shiny and flat at range: new jp_m_roof_kawara_far for the Resolution 2 and 3 fields.
       - It is matte: 0.22/35 against 0.6/90.
       - The LOD0 corrugation and course shadows are baked into colour and normal map.
       - Its mean is 4 L* darker (dE 3-12/14).
       - The Resolution 3 field is one quad per slope.
       - Check: C16.
   (b) Ridge-end ornaments popping: every LOD now keeps the ridge stack (Resolution 2 and 3 blocks), the ridge ends,
       the onigawara (two prisms and its back block), the verge strips, the eave strip and the udatsu cap courses.
       Before, the Resolution 3 ridge cap floated 0.17 m over nothing. Check: C15 (Resolution 2/3 top heights within
       0.10 m of Resolution 1).
   (c) "Something on the side clips into the roof": the tile gable's board band and rail were full rectangles that
       rose through the roof at both eave corners, in LOD 1-2 only. They are now clipped 2 cm under the roof lines
       (walls.gable). This was also what Stephen saw on the parts sheet (2b gables). Check: C12 (5 roof bodies clear).
   Also: the flashing strips' invisible caps were dropped (-39 faces per LOD).
