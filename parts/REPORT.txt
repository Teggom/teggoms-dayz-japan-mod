  - Extra: jp_p_frame_dashigeta_upper_front (G1 decision 4 asks for projecting upper fronts as an optional

By group (src/JP/parts/<group>/, = P:\JP\parts\...):
  frame 6, wall 20, open 35, found 11, porch 3, roof 52, trim 2.

Per variant:
  - <name>.p3d: MLOD with every LOD it contributes (Res 1-3, Geometry, Memory, Roadway, View Geometry, Fire
    Geometry).
  - <name>.part.json: the PLAYBOOK §10.2 sidecar. It holds dims, tiers, materials with the wear level selectable
    per instance, LOD face counts, connectors, doors, the build-list reference and its checks.
  - One checks.json per group.
  - parts/manifest.json lists all 129.

The code is in parts/kit/ (python package jpparts, grown from B's jpkit):
  core.py       Solid / Part / LOD writer. Materials and UVs are resolved in the part's frame, so a part can be moved,
                rotated or mirrored without its textures sliding. Wear is chosen per instance (Part.wear,
                Part.wear_by_mat).
  shapes.py     Helpers: convex slabs over plan polygons, tubes, clipping, convex stones, board runs.
  frame.py      Posts, keta / nuki / dodai / beam ends, dashigeta.
  walls.py      Wall RECIPES (wall_run for any half-ken run, openings as holes), wainscot, namako, gable, udatsu.
  openings.py   Sliding doors (B's translation format), kura doors (rotation), windows, lattices, shop closures.
  found.py      Soseki, dodai on stones, kura footing, yukashita, steps with hidden ramps, engawa, nure-en.
  kawara.py     Kawara as geometry (see below).
  roofs.py      The roof generator: footprint x form x family -> slopes -> coverings + collision.
  roofparts.py  The roof entries built with the generator.
  trim.py       The grime decal band.
  checks.py     C2 C3 C4 C5 C7.
  registry.py   The priority-ordered list of every variant.
Drivers:
  build_parts.py        Writes all p3ds, sidecars, checks and the manifest (about 3 s).
  render_sheets.py      Contact sheets, via render_parts.py in background Blender.
  assembly.py           The test assembly, MLOD merge and binarize.
  make_part_materials.py  The two new library materials.
  prestep_palette.py    The palette edits.

Key conventions (manifest.json "frame"/"standards"):
  - Part frame: origin at the left post centreline, at finished floor / sill level. +x runs along the wall, +y up,
    +z is the exterior.
    - "Left" is as seen standing inside, facing out; seen from outside, +x runs to the viewer's left.
    - autocenter=0.
  - Every post, bay and opening edge is on the 0.455 grid; walls span post node to post node.
  - Standard heights: sill 0, door head 2.00, keta underside 2.70, eave line (keta top) 2.88.
  - Walls are recipes: walls.wall_run(part, kind, x0, x1, openings=[...]) fills ANY half-ken run.
    - It draws internal posts at ken nodes. End posts come from jp_p_frame_post.
    - Opening parts fill the holes it leaves.
    - The p3d samples are 1-ken runs.
  - Roofs are generated from the footprint: roofs.roof(part, W, D, form, family).
    - Forms: kirizuma / yosemune / irimoya / kabuto.
    - Families: sangawara / hongawara / ishioki / itabuki / kakigara / thatch.
    - The eave line is the keta top. Kawara columns are 0.26 m (ken/7), so a column edge lands on every ken line.
    - Every slope is split into convex pieces; each piece has one collision slab.
  - Doors are in B's proven format: type translation, one bone per leaf, memory axis exactly 1.00 m, an action
    point and a leaf point, doorWoodSlide sounds.
    - Every passable door is a 1-ken bay: 1.70 m clear between the post faces.
    - Its single leaf parks along the facade over the NEXT bay: the 'park' connector, which must be plain wall on
      that face.
    - The part carries its tracks over the park bay.

Kawara as geometry (rule 1). This is the v1 machiya failure; the close-ups show it now reads as tiles.
  - The field is corrugated in 0.26 columns: 4 segments per column at LOD0, 2 at LOD1, flat at LOD2.
  - Modelled courses (top + butt faces): 2 rows at the eave and 1 at the ridge.
  - The continuous middle carries the course lines through a new library texture (see 3).
  - Eave tiles have a lip and a round manju end; the tomoe variant has a raised boss.
  - Sode-gawara verge tiles run along gables.
  - Noshi ridges: 3 or 5 courses on shikkui bedding, with a round ganburi cap and end tiles.
  - Hip ridges; onigawara (plain, and 'sui' in relief).
  - Hongawara: flat pans plus round covers at 0.303.
Thatch has real thickness.
  - The eave is square-cut 0.60 in three stepped bands (thatch_cut texture), with hip rolls.
  - Ridges: bamboo, tiled, turf with iris, and umanori.
Stone-weighted roofs have split-pole battens every 0.60 up the slope and river stones from an 8-shape set on their
upslope side.

3. PRE-STEP CHOICES
-------------------
1. kawara_ibushi darker: (145,149,149) -> (118,122,123) in playbook/palette.json.
   - Why: B's in-game-approved machiya roof texture averages (81,83,83). With the new entry the recipe's _w1
     (-8 L) lands at mean ~101, inside the 95-110 target.
     - _w0 (new tiles) is ~121.
     - _w2's offset went from -4 to -9 L so heavy wear stays near normal (mean ~101) instead of lighter.
   - method "calibrated", the photo value kept as photo_srgb, note "in-engine calibration, Stephen approved the
     machiya roof in game".
   - build_materials.py got an --only option. I ran it for kawara, kureita and kokera: 27 PAAs, sidecars,
     matcheck over all 66 sets (0 FAIL; 1 old WARN on thatch_cut_w0), jp_common.pbo repacked, material sheets
     refreshed.
   - matcheck: kawara w0 dE 0.9, w1 8.1, w2 8.3 (tol 14).
2. board_new (156,122,84), tol 6.
   - Source: fresh hinoki board, sampled from the CC0 Poly Haven "Hinoki Planks" scan (B's machiya floor source).
   - No build-list or playbook photo shows fresh boards in usable light. x01_ioka_a has one new board, but in
     blue evening shade.
   - It is now the _w0 of jp_m_roof_kureita and jp_m_roof_kokera (PALETTE_BY_WEAR): kureita w0 dE 0.0,
     kokera w0 dE 2.0.
   - Weathering line to roof_board_silver.
3. Grime strip.
   - New palette entry grime_splash (104,89,71), sampled on x08_hirose_earthwall: the bottom band of the earth
     panels, which is ~12 % darker than the same panels 0.1 m higher.
   - New material jp_m_wall_grime (w0 dust / w1 splash / w2 mud with moss).
     - An alpha decal (_ca, DXT5) with the vanilla decal rvmat pattern (renderFlags NoZWrite).
     - u tiles every 2 m along the wall; v spans a 0.45 m band.
     - The RGB is the grime colour everywhere, so the matcheck mean is meaningful: w0 dE 4.0, w1 0.0, w2 5.0
       (tol 6).
   - Part jp_p_trim_grime_wall / _post: sheets 3 mm proud of the face, never in Geometry.
Also added, beyond the brief: jp_m_roof_kawara_field (palette kawara_ibushi, dE as kawara).
  - It is the same ceramic with the 0.235 courses (AO under each butt, lip highlight, a step in the normal map) and
    a per-tile tint (W8).
  - Tile: 4 columns x 4 courses = 1.04 x 0.94 m.
  - Why: PLAYBOOK §6.1 says the rows between the modelled eave and ridge rows come "from the normal map and baked
    AO", and the library kawara has no rows. Without it the middle of a roof would read as corrugated sheet.
Script: parts/kit/make_part_materials.py; results in src/JP/common/materials/checks_parts.json.
jp_common.pbo (@Japanddons, 100.2 MB) is repacked and includes every new or changed PAA and rvmat
(kawara, kureita, kokera, grime, kawara_field).
Grime tuning: the splash reaches ~0.30 m, with a ragged top. On earth walls it is a subtle ~10 % darkening, as
sampled on x08. On shikkui and on dark timber it reads clearly (sheet 7 shows it on shikkui).


4. DEVIATIONS FROM THE BUILD LIST (and why)
-------------------------------------------
- itado _pair: built on a 1.5-ken bay, not 1 ken. In a 1-ken pair each leaf opens 0.85 m, below D1. On 1.5 ken each
  leaf gives 1.26 m.
- All passable sliding doors (itado, shoji_ext, koshido, suriagedo) use one 1.74 m leaf per 1-ken bay (1.70 m
  clear).
  - Real leaves were ~0.9 m, but D1 (>= 1.00 m, never parked across a passage) rules out the period two-leaf bay.
  - Shoji are drawn as two panels on one leaf. See decision 1.
- mushiko _oval_pair: each oval is 0.70 x 0.40 so two fit one 1-ken bay (the build list gives 0.90 x 0.45 for the
  single oval, and that is kept).
- engawa clear width is 1.245 m.
  - The outer post line is on the 0.455 grid (z 1.365); the build list says 1.00-1.20.
  - The grid wins; D5 is satisfied.
- shinkabe infill setback is 0.0225 each side: a 0.075 infill centred in a 0.12 post. The build list gives
  0.02 (A); both cannot hold at once.
- ishioki boards: the library kureita texture has 0.25 courses. It is laid at 1.2 m so its courses are the build
  list's 0.15 (board widths come out 0.07-0.18).
- namako: joint pitch 0.2495 (tile 0.2145 + joint 0.035) as the build list says. The namako texture is laid at
  0.998 m so its tiles line up with the raised joints; _shihan rotates the UVs 45 deg.
- suriagedo boards are 0.667 high (2.00 / 3, the head is 2.00). The build list says ~0.68 (2.04 / 3).
- kura door: the opening is 1.10 x 2.15 from the approach, i.e. 2.00 clear above the 0.15 raised threshold.
  - The wall recipe must leave that hole.
  - The outer plaster leaves are static open in _open.
  - In _hinged they are rotation doors (angle 100 deg): ENGINE-UNTESTED, and the axis direction may need
    swapping.
- suriagedo _door: 3 boards on one door source, vertical translation into a hollow box above the head (each board on
  its own groove track). ENGINE-UNTESTED; _closed and _part are the static fall-backs.
- dashigeta _std + _upper_front: G1 decision 4 deviation (post-1750), flagged in their sidecars, tier 3 only.
- Roofs:
  - Tile and board slopes and pents carry Roadway (walkable); thatch (45 deg) does not. See decision 2.
  - kabuto is modelled as a tall smoke gable at one end and a hip at the other.
- Every roof sample uses the kit's standard eave line 2.88 (keta top).
- Pent heights:
  - Street pent: attaches at 3.30, eave 2.94.
  - Skirt pent for thatch entrances (G1 decision 6): attaches at 2.70, projects 1.20 on posts, soffit >= 2.30.
Playbook conflicts: none beyond the D1 / D5 interactions above.


5. DECISIONS FOR STEPHEN (G2)
-----------------------------
1. Door leaves. Every walk-through sliding door is one 1.74 m leaf in a 1-ken bay (1.70 m clear), parking over the
   next bay. That is right for a big plank oodo, but wide for shoji and lattice doors (period ~0.9 m).
   - Keep, or
   - use 1.5-ken bays with a fixed half-ken panel and a 1.30 m leaf (still >= 1.00 m clear, looks more period)?
2. Walkable roofs. Tile and board roofs and street pents have Roadway, so players (and loot) can be on them.
   Keep, or make roofs non-walkable?
3. Roof look (sheets 5a and 6, and the close-ups on sheet 8).
   - Approve kawara geometry plus the new jp_m_roof_kawara_field course texture?
   - Confirm the darker kawara (_w1 mean ~101 vs the machiya's 82) at the next in-game check.
4. Dashigeta and the projecting upper front (sheet 1, G1 decision 4). Keep both as optional tier-3 parts, or drop
   the projecting front?
5. Hip and irimoya tile roofs are ~4.0-4.4k faces at LOD0 on a 4 x 3 ken footprint (the 3k budget is met by
   kirizuma). Accept for inns and big houses, or cut the modelled eave rows from 2 to 1 on hipped tile roofs
   (about -900 faces)?
