## Made here (no third-party content)

These were all generated by the scripts in `spikes/F_flora/tools/`:

- **Sakura blossom atlas** and its wind mask, drawn procedurally by `textures.py`: 5-petal notched flowers, buds, pedicels and twigs.
- **Bamboo leaf atlas** and its wind mask, drawn procedurally by `textures.py`.
- **Bamboo culm textures**, procedural: `jp_bamboo_culm_co` and `jp_bamboo_culm_nohq`, with periodic noise, node rings and wax bands.
- **Bamboo pole textures**, procedural: `jp_bamboo_pole_co` and `jp_bamboo_pole_nohq`.
- **LOD4 impostor textures**, rendered in Blender from our own LOD1 meshes.
- **All geometry**, made with procedural Python: the trees, the clump and the pole.

## Vanilla references (read only, nothing copied into the PBO)

These vanilla DayZ files were used only as references:

- **rvmat parameter blocks**, taken as plain-text values:
  - `t_prunusdomestica_2s_*.rvmat`
  - `t_larixdecidua_3f_trunk.rvmat`
  - `t_betulapendula_2s_leaves.rvmat`
  - `b_corylusavellana_*.rvmat`
  - `wooden_stick*.rvmat`
- **LOD layout, named properties and memory-point names**, read from the binarized strings of:
  - `t_prunusdomestica_2s.p3d`
  - `b_corylusavellana_2s.p3d`
  - `wooden_stick_blunt.p3d`
- **Materials still referenced at run time** from the game's own data. The PBO references these but does not ship them:
  - `dz\data\data\penetration\wood.rvmat` and `dz\data\data\penetration\foliage.rvmat`
  - `dz\characters\data\generic_wood_damage_mc.paa` and `dz\characters\data\generic_destruct_mc.paa`
  - `dz\data\data\env_land_co.paa`
