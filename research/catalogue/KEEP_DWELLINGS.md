# KEEP list: dwellings (review sitting 1, Stephen + lead, 2026-09-29)

**Status:** APPROVED by Stephen, 2026-09-29.

This collapses `research/buildings/BUILDING_LIST.md`: its dwelling sections (poor, lower-middle, upper, outbuildings;
137 home types plus 21 outbuilding kinds) and its §6.1 suggestions. There are three layers:
- **Shell:** a distinct building model. This is what costs work.
- **Variant:** parts-kit swaps: roof material (thatch / board / tile), door and window styles, size or frontage.
  Cheap: a script run.
- **Dressing:** prop sets that say who lives there.

Loot tiers are ignored for now (Stephen, 2026-09-29).

**Target:** 30 shells × about 3 variants ≈ 90 distinct-looking homes. Chernarus has about 52 home models, many of them
recolours.

## Cut, folded or moved

- **Cut (off-map regional forms):** gasshō, chūmon L-house, Nanbu magariya, kabuto silk house, Kai takahe house (also
  later than 1730), the other far regional forms, boat dwelling (ebune).
- **Cut (become props or nothing):** log storehouse (azekura), dug cellar, ash shed, hen coop, manure shed, cremation
  attendant's hut, the Danzaemon compound.
- **Folded:** servants' quarters are rooms inside other shells, not buildings. Retirement cottage = hermit hut or a
  small shell-1/30 variant. Religious families (shake, farmer-priest, yamabushi household) = headman or farm shells
  with a ritual prop set. The oshi pilgrim house goes to the lodging sitting.
- **Moved to later sittings:** honjin family rooms → lodging; Osaka kura-yashiki → trades; honmaru goten → military.

## How the cities are built (for the map work)

A city block (chō, about 120 m square) has machiya (shells 11/12) and street-front rows (13) along its street
frontages, back-alley tenements (3) filling its interior behind a kido gate, and kura (22) at the back of the
machiya lots. Modular units give many buildings from few shells, like Chernarus's houseblocks (667 placed from 20
models).
