a few unique parts. Sources: `research/landmarks/LANDMARKS_RANKED.md`, `E_EDO_TOKAIDO.md` (E ids),

**The world is DayZ, not a living city** (Stephen): towns are dead and empty like Chernarus. There are no lords,
crowds or open shops; maybe random bandits later. Landmarks show the place **as it was left**.

## Stephen's calls (2026-09-29)

1. **Volcanoes: Fuji, Asama AND Ontake**, all as terrain. The routes pass by them.
   - **Fuji:** the playable site (crater, pilgrim huts)
   - **Asama:** smoking in 1730, with ash eruptions recorded in 1729 and 1731; the 1783 lava field is NOT there yet
   - **Ontake:** a volcano, yes; the Kiso pilgrim mountain (mass pilgrimage only from 1785)
2. **Checkpoints: four.** Hakone (E40), Arai (E77, the coast road), Kiso-Fukushima (W92, the mountain road), Usui
   (W107, the east entry). All the checkpoint kit.
3. **The middle of the map = the real Kai / Kōfu basin** (as map v2).
4. **Everything between the routes and the sea is real and in. Only land outside the map (north, east, west) is cut.**
   - **Nara:** IN. On the map, about 4 km from the SW corner. Tōdai-ji (hall rebuilt 1709), Kōfuku-ji (half-burned
     1717, pagoda standing).
   - **Ise:** IN. On the map, on the Ise Bay coast. Ise Grand Shrine, rebuilt 1729, so its halls are brand new in 1730.
   - **Kōya:** IN, NUDGED. Its real spot falls 1.3 km past the south edge, so it goes just inside the south-west
     corner ("close is ok").
5. **Fun one-offs:**
   - **Edo elephant: OUT.** It was a real, live elephant, and there are no NPCs.
   - **Nishijin burn scar** (a burned-out weaving district, summer 1730): **ignored for now.** "We can always burn
     the town later."
   - **Daimyo processions:** a gameplay idea, not a build.

## Dead-world dressing (applies to every landmark and dressing set)
Everything is shown **abandoned**: no people. Stalls are left standing with scattered goods. Shops have shutters
half-open or broken. Festival sets are remnants. Castles and checkpoints are unmanned. This fits the "damage / ruin
states" multiplier noted in KEEP_OUTDOOR, and is decided per dressing set at its build list.
