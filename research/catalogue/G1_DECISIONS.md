   outdoors, but accept that as suspension of disbelief: keep them, weathered.
2. **Wells and notice board** are built once here and reused by KEEP_CIVIC; the roofed well (dwelling 28) is a variant:
   YES.
3. **Wells work as vanilla drink and wash wells:** YES.
4. **Jizō bibs,** faded, on about 1 in 3: YES.
5. **Readable text** from a free (SIL OFL) brush font: YES, and it must be **JAPANESE** (a Japanese brush / kaisho
   face, OFL-licensed).
6. **Handcart** as the abandoned-vehicle prop: YES, **several variants at different levels of disrepair**.
7. **Festival layer:** banners and lanterns now, floats and stages later. All weathered and messed up: YES.
8. **Shop-front dressing** as proxies on the shop building: YES.
9. **Face budget:** 1,500 is the CEILING ("if you need it all"), not a target. Simple items use far less. Large objects
   may go higher, but they're usually composites of smaller models anyway (bridges). Detail can be deepened later on
   request.
10. **Moss** as a separate decal material: YES.
11. **Village torii** are "standard", not "hero" (PLAYBOOK §11 research depth): YES.
