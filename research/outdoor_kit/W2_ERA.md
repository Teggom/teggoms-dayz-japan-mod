# W2 era check: torii, lanterns, basins, steps and graveyard stones (agent W2, 2026-09-30)

The test (PLAYBOOK §1): did it exist, or still stand, in 1730? Older survivors are IN. This file covers the 7 W2 items
of `BUILD_LIST.md` and Stephen's additions (torii with/without rope and shide, mossy torii and lanterns, the graveyard
at 2-3x variety).

**Sources.** `[Oxx]` and `[kxx]` are the build list's references (`refs_index.json`), `[S1xx]` the OUTDOOR_LIST seams.
No web requests were made in this session; items marked **(knowledge)** rest on the standard literature named in the
row and were not re-read online today. **(assumed)** means no source; the reason is given.

## What this means for the build
- Graveyard mix for the placement agent (list `mix_1730` + the additions): board 30 %, boat-halo 25 %, round 12 %,
  field stones / mounds 15 %, Jizo child 5 %, square pillar 5 %, gorinto / hokyointo 8 %. Rows irregular: stones on
  the 0.9 m plot grid but jittered +-0.15 m and +-12 deg, mixed heights, gaps where a plot was never stoned.
- Text only from B1's carved-text atlas (two posthumous names, nenbutsu, Jizo); gorinto carry no text (their Sanskrit
  seed syllables would need new atlas cells: listed as missing in W2_PROGRESS.md).
