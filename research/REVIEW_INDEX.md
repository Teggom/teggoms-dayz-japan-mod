# Review index: the 1730 world research (lead, 2026-09-29)

**Update 2026-09-29:** all five sittings are done. The results are in `catalogue/KEEP_*.md`, and the next work is in
`../PRODUCTION_PLAN.md`.

Start here. Everything below is TEXT research, deliberately over-detailed so Stephen can collapse it. Nothing is
decided until a KEEP list says so.

**The era rule** (PLAYBOOK §1): the test is "did it exist, or still stand, in 1730". Old survivors are in. Tags mark
only what didn't exist yet or was already gone.

**The loot rule:** furniture and props are dressing. Loot lies out on floors and surfaces, like vanilla DayZ.
Containers are rare.

## Where the lists need a ruling or a check

- **The gap audit's top gaps** (`GAP_AUDIT.md` §1): the Kai/Kōfu basin, north Fuji, the Kōshū road, market-town and
  salt-village layouts, the Mino pilgrim sites, Suwa/Ina, Kawachi and the Settsu coast. All are written up in §3,
  ready to add after review.
- **Corrections** (`GAP_AUDIT.md` §5): Osaka's execution ground was Tobita, not Sennichi; kanpyō belongs at
  Minakuchi (on our map); Naitō-Shinjuku did not exist in 1730.
- **Contradictions:** `BUILDING_LIST.md` §4.2 (14, e.g. the iron kettle, zabuton, inn bath tubs) and
  `OUTDOOR_LIST.md` §3.3 (e.g. the always-lit lantern, stone Inari foxes, komainu in villages, fire tubs, fire
  ladders). These matter only for items we keep, so check them after the collapse, not before.
- **Verify flags:** 65 in buildings (§7), about 300 lines in outdoor (§8). Same rule: only check what survives the
  collapse.

## The review sittings (Stephen + lead, one category at a time)

Each sitting ends in a written KEEP list and a variant target for that category.

1. **Dwellings**: the biggest share, and where most of the model budget goes (Chernarus: 60% of buildings are homes)
2. **Trades and workshops**, with the meibutsu index as the guide to which trades our map actually needs
3. **Civic, religious and military**
4. **Outdoor kit**: the setting kits (`OUTDOOR_LIST.md` §4) and the period traps (§6)
5. **Landmarks**: `LANDMARKS_RANKED.md`'s top 20 and its 5 decisions

The KEEP lists then become the v1 catalogue, replacing the rough v1 in `playbook/WORLD_CATALOGUE.md` §3.
