# Life layer: the draft list (lead, 2026-09-30) — FOR STEPHEN TO PICK AFTER G4

**Goal:** about 3x the prop VARIETY (not 3x the props in each house), so the houses and streets feel lived in and
then abandoned. Today: 24 interior + 20 outdoor = 44 items. This list adds **50 interior + 24 outdoor = 74 items**
(118 in total, about 2.7x). The shop dressing sets in the last section can take it past 3x.

**The rule that makes it fit:** our binding sparseness rules (5-7 props a room, at most 25% of the floor, a 1 m clear
band) stay. Most of the new life hangs on walls, posts and beams, or sits on existing surfaces. So a room gets
richer without getting more crowded and without eating loot space.

**How to read it:**
- **Count** = how many period building entries name the item (research/buildings/BUILDING_LIST.md §5).
- ★ = already researched in A2 (research/interior/BUILD_LIST.md, wave 2), so it's ready to build.
- **Where:** wall, beam, post, surface (on a shelf, chest or tray), floor, yard, street.
- **Tier:** T1 poor/rural, T2 village/post town, T3 town/merchant, U = upper only (samurai, headman, honjin).
- ⚠ = era check needed before building (the 1730 test).
- Excluded on purpose: the tetsubin iron kettle (A2 ruling), the beckoning cat (after 1750), tokonoma dressing in
  commoner houses (G1).

## Beyond 3x: the shop dressing sets
KEEP_TRADES has **22 townhouse shop dressing sets** (goods for a rice shop, sake, cloth, paper, pots, medicine and so
on). Each is a swap of the goods on B3a's shop stand, and together they give every shop a different trade. They
were always planned for Phase C. Pulling them forward would roughly double the SHOP variety on its own.

- Outdoor 24: one agent, about 0.5M tokens, 40 minutes, ~1-2% weekly.
- The ⚠ era checks first: a short research pass (or the building agent checks each before modelling).
