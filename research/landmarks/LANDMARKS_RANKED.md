## Decisions only Stephen can make

1. **Volcano:** Fuji only, Asama only, or both? The layout has one Hakone-style volcano. Fuji is the obvious skyline;
   Asama suits the inland road.
2. **How many checkpoints:** the real corridor has Hakone, Arai, Kiso-Fukushima, Usui and Kobotoke. The layout calls
   for two passes with checkpoints.
3. **The middle of the map:** the draft layout puts a "central range" between the roads, but the real land there is
   the Kai basin and the Suwa–Ina valleys (the gap audit's #1). Keep the invented range, or use the real basin?
4. **Off-map extras:** Nara, Ise, Kōya — cut, or squeeze in one?
5. **Fun one-offs from the research:**
   - the Edo elephant (lived at Hama-goten 1729–42)
   - Nishijin's 1730 burn scar
   - the 1730 swing back to full daimyo processions
   
   Worth a nod on the map?
