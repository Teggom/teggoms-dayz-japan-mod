# Stephen's wave-2 walk, 2026-10-01 (verbatim notes, lightly formatted)

P (town shrine):
- The leaves on the donation (offering) box are weirdly draped on it. Nowhere else nearby has leaves; why would the box? And the box blocks the stairs into the shrine.
- P1 shrine doors that open: the handles are attached to the opposite door (the right door takes the left door's handle with it, and vice versa); the handle floats.
- P5: masks etc. on the wall are too low resolution.
- General: small detail items get the smallest face allotment possible, but they are what make the world pop (from the rope to other props): give them more. Buildings take a lot of shapes, fine, but small props keep getting ignored.
- Everything else in P looks good.

- t4 swings open good.
- t5: the statue is a blob. Statues need another pass: any humanoid statue should have a picture reference and more care. Even the Jizo statues are bare bones. The Jizo's staff has a see-through top.
